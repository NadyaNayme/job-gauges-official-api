# Job Gauges

Overlays that help you track important resources for every combat style. No more trying to quickly hunt
where your stacks are on your Buffs Bar - everything important to keep track of exists in one convenient place: your job gauge.

Job Gauges is intentionally created as an *opinionated* plugin. It tracks what is deemed to be important for meta, endgame PVM strategies and development time is not spent on allowing users to track arbitrary things like adding their own weapon special attacks.

Please do not PR new components without consulting me, as it will likely be rejected otherwise. PR's attempting to make Job Gauges **less opinionated** will be rejected. If you want to recreate Job Gauges but for arbitrary weapon specials and abilities - please create your own plugin. To reiterate: Job gauges is **_intentionally opinionated_** in what is tracked.
